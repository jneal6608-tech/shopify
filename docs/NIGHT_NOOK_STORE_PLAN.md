# Night Nook — Store Blueprint (nightnookstore.com)

Research-backed plan for building the Shopify store around two motion-sensor night lights.
Goal: when someone arrives from a TikTok / Reels / Shorts video, they buy.

---

## 1. Realistic expectations (so you can judge your numbers)

| Metric | Typical range | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify median conversion rate | ~1.4% | ~1–2 sales per 100 visitors |
| Home & furniture stores | ~1.2–1.9% | Your category's normal range |
| TikTok traffic | ~0.3–1.5% (1%+ is solid) | Social visitors browse; the page must convince fast |

Sources: Shopify's benchmark guide via [Blend Commerce](https://blendcommerce.com/blogs/shopify/ecommerce-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2026), [GrowthSuite](https://www.growthsuite.net/resources/shopify-conversion-rate/traffic-source-conversion/tiktok-traffic), [Kinect](https://trykinect.ai/resources/convert-tiktok-traffic-shopify). These are mostly vendor blogs; use them as direction, not promises.

**So:** your first goal is ~100–300 visitors per day from videos. At 1%, that's 1–3 orders/day. The page's job is to push you toward 1.5–2%.

---

## 2. The big ideas that drive conversion (in priority order)

1. **Message match.** The page must continue the exact promise of the video. If the video says "never stub your toe at 3am", the first thing on the page says the same thing ([Kinect](https://trykinect.ai/resources/convert-tiktok-traffic-shopify)).
2. **Show it working, in the dark.** A short looping demo video near the top beats paragraphs of text ([RevenueFlows](https://revenueflows.ai/blog/how-to-write-shopify-product-page-for-tiktok-traffic)).
3. **Shipping and returns visible on the product page.** 64% of shoppers look for shipping info on the product page, yet 43% of sites hide it ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux)). Hiding long dropshipping delivery times leads to refunds and chargebacks ($15–100 fee each) ([Tradelle](https://www.tradelle.io/es/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-dropshipping-chargebacks/)).
4. **Size in context.** 42% of shoppers judge size from images, so include an "in scale" photo (light in a hand, next to a phone) ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/in-scale-product-images)).
5. **Fast, mobile-first, one clear button.** Nearly all visitors are on phones. Big Add to Cart, sticky on scroll, Shop Pay / Apple Pay on ([OnTap](https://www.ontapgroup.com/blog/shopify-conversion-rate-optimization)).
6. **Bundles / pack sizes.** Pack options raise order value without lowering your base price ([Adoric](https://adoric.com/blog/?p=14279)). Our 3-pack-first pricing does this.
7. **Honest trust only.** No fake reviews, fake countdown timers, fake "23 people viewing", or invented "was" prices. The FTC's fake-review rule (in force since Oct 2024) allows civil penalties ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)). Do **not** import AliExpress reviews as if they were your customers'.

## 4. Theme & look

**Theme: Dawn (free).** It's the fastest free Shopify theme and keeps images front and centre ([Let's Talk Shop](https://www.letstalkshop.com/blog/best-free-shopify-themes)). Horizon is the alternative if you want more layout blocks ([Shopify](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/horizon)). Start with Dawn; speed matters most for TikTok traffic.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Background (scheme 1) | Midnight navy `#141B2D` |
| Text on dark | Cream `#F6EFE6` |
| Buttons / accents | Warm amber `#F2A65A` (dark text on button) |
| Secondary accent | Dusty lavender `#9C8FB8` |
| Light sections (scheme 2) | Cream background `#F6EFE6`, navy text — use for specs/FAQ so long text is easy to read |
| Headings font | A soft serif from Shopify's font picker (e.g. Lora or Playfair Display) |
| Body font | A clean sans (e.g. Inter / Assistant / DM Sans) |
| Logo | Lowercase "night nook" + small crescent moon in a doorway arch, amber on navy |
| Announcement bar | "Free shipping on every order · 30-day money-back guarantee" |

Vibe: lamplight in a dark room — dark pages, warm glowing product shots, calm copy.

## 6. Hero product page — top to bottom (mobile)

**Above the fold (first screen on a phone)**
1. **Image/video gallery** — first slide = 5–8 s looping clip: dark hallway, feet step out of bed, light fades on.
2. **Title:** "Night Nook Motion Night Light"
3. **Outcome line (message match):** "No more stubbed toes on 3am bathroom trips."
4. **Rating stars** — only once you have real reviews. Until then, leave them off.
5. **Price + pack selector** — 1 / **3 (Most popular, preselected)** / 6.
6. **Add to cart** (amber, full width) + **Shop Pay / Apple Pay** buttons.
7. **Delivery line right under the button:** "🚚 Free shipping · arrives in 7–12 business days" (use your real average).
8. **3 icon bullets:** "Lights up when you walk by · Peel, stick, done — no wiring · USB-C rechargeable".

**Below the fold**
9. **Problem → solution:** dark photo + "Fumbling for the switch in the dark?" → "Night Nook lights the way the second you move — then turns itself off."
10. **How it works (3 steps):** Charge (USB-C) → Stick or magnet-mount → Walk by, it glows.
11. **Where people use it (photo grid):** bedside · hallway · bathroom · closet · kids' room · under cabinets.
12. **Specs (cream section):** size, sensor range ~10 ft / 120°, auto-off ~20 s, warm white, battery life & charge time (**measure these on your sample — don't guess**), what's in the box.
13. **Comparison table:** Night Nook vs plug-in night lights (no outlet needed, only on when needed, place anywhere).
14. **Our promise:** 30-day money-back guarantee · real support at hello@nightnookstore.com.
15. **FAQ (accordion):** Is it bright enough? How long does a charge last? Does it work in daylight? Can I stick it on any wall? Will it damage paint? How long is delivery? What if it arrives damaged?
16. **Reviews section** (Judge.me) — empty at first; fills from post-purchase review emails.
17. **"Complete the setup"** — cross-sell the Light Bar and the Night Path Kit.
18. **Sticky Add to Cart** bar on scroll (Dawn setting / small app).

Same template for the Light Bar page, with the angle: "The closet / under-bed / cabinet light that turns itself on."

## 11. What to measure after launch (Shopify Analytics)

| Funnel step | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Sessions from TikTok / IG / YouTube | Are videos sending visitors? |
| Add-to-cart rate | Low → page/price isn't convincing; test headline, first video, price |
| Reached checkout → purchased | Low → shipping cost/time surprise or trust issue |
| Conversion rate | Aim for 1%+, then push toward 1.5–2% |
| Which pack sells | If most buy 1-packs, make the 3-pack value clearer |

Change one thing at a time (headline, first gallery clip, default pack) and give it ~3–7 days of traffic before judging.

Log your numbers weekly in Dropship Intel → Testing Workspace so the platform learns what works.
