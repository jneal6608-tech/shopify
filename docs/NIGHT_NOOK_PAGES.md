## 2. Refund policy (30-day guarantee)

Shopify → **Settings → Policies → Return and refund policy**

> **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**
>
> We want you to love your lights. If you don't, you have **30 days from delivery** to ask for a refund.
>
> **Damaged, faulty or not working?**
> Email hello@nightnookstore.com with your order number and a photo or short video. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund — **no need to send anything back.**
>
> **Changed your mind?**
> Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number. We'll reply with return instructions. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Return shipping is paid by the customer for change-of-mind returns. Your refund is issued as soon as the return arrives.
>
> **Refund timing:** Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank usually takes 5–10 business days to show it.
>
> **Exchanges:** Want a different pack size or product? Email us and we'll sort it out.
>
> **How to start:** hello@nightnookstore.com — include your order number.

> **Your note (don't paste):** for a cheap change-of-mind order, refunding without asking for the item back is often cheaper than handling a return. You can always be more generous than the policy.

## 5. Pages

Shopify → **Online Store → Pages → Add page**. Title as shown, paste the text, **Save**.

### FAQ  (title: `FAQ`)

> **How does the motion sensor work?**
> The light turns on automatically when it senses movement in the dark, then turns itself off after about [20 seconds — check sample] of no motion. It stays off in daylight so the battery lasts longer.
>
> **How bright is it?**
> Bright enough to see where you're walking, soft enough not to wake you up. It's a warm white glow — not a harsh blue light.
>
> **How do I charge it?**
> With the included USB cable. A full charge takes about [X hours — check sample].
>
> **How long does a charge last?**
> It depends how often it's triggered. With normal use (a few trips a night) one charge lasts about [X weeks — check sample].
>
> **How do I mount it?**
> Peel the adhesive backing, stick the magnetic plate where you want it, and snap the light on. Lift it off the magnet any time to charge it. No tools, no wiring.
>
> **Will it damage my walls?**
> The adhesive holds well on smooth, clean surfaces. Like most adhesives it can lift paint on some walls if removed — try a less visible spot first if you're worried.
>
> **Where should I put it?**
> Hallways, bedside, stairs, closets, bathrooms, under cabinets — anywhere you walk in the dark.
>
> **How long is delivery?**
> Free shipping to the US. Most orders arrive in 7–12 business days, with tracking.
>
> **What if it arrives damaged or doesn't work?**
> Email us a photo and we'll replace it or refund you — no need to send it back.
>
> **Can I return it?**
> Yes. You have 30 days from delivery. See our Refund Policy.
